Our calculation method

Canadian nominal annual interest converted from semi-annual compounding: periodic rate = (1 + annual rate / 200)2 / payments per year − 1. Each payment is applied to interest first, then principal. The difference between standard and accelerated schedules is explicitly modelled.

For insured mortgages, the estimate assumes a standard CMHC owner-occupied purchase premium, typically financed, with a provisional 0.20 percentage-point extension for eligible 30-year insured amortizations. Ontario's 8% insurance sales tax is treated as an upfront cost. It excludes rebate eligibility, unique insurer pricing and taxes outside Ontario.

Toronto's 2026 high-value municipal land transfer brackets are used for typical 1–2-unit residential property estimates. First-time buyer rebates, non-resident taxes, and other special circumstances are excluded.
