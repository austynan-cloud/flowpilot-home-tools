How land transfer tax works

Ontario charges land transfer tax using graduated rates based on the property's purchase price.

Properties located in the City of Toronto are generally subject to an additional municipal land transfer tax.

This simplified calculator excludes first-time buyer rebates, special exemptions, non-resident taxes, and other adjustments. Toronto residential purchases of $3 million or more require additional municipal tax brackets and are not supported by this estimator.

Verify rates and eligibility using the official Ontario guidance and Toronto municipal guidance.
