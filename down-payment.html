How minimum down payments work in Canada

For eligible homes below $500,000, the minimum down payment is generally 5%.

For homes costing $500,000 to less than $1.5 million, the minimum is 5% of the first $500,000 plus 10% of the remaining amount.

For homes costing $1.5 million or more, a down payment of at least 20% is generally required.

A down payment below 20% commonly requires mortgage default insurance, subject to eligibility. Lenders may require a larger down payment.

Important information

This calculator provides educational estimates, not mortgage approval or financial advice. It excludes mortgage insurance premiums, closing costs, taxes, and other expenses. Confirm current requirements with a qualified lender.
