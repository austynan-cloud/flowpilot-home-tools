04 / Check the assumptions

Clear maths. No mystery approval scores.

Canadian compounding

For fixed-rate mortgage modelling, a quoted nominal rate compounded semi-annually is converted to the chosen payment interval. Both zero-interest and accelerated-frequency cases are supported.

Insurance & tax caveats

Standard CMHC owner-occupied loan premium bands are estimated and financed. Ontario's 8% insurance premium sales tax is upfront cash. The model excludes first-time buyer rebates, special taxes, insurer-specific rules and HST on new homes.

Not lender preapproval

A stress-test payment is illustrative only. Credit, household income, debts, eligibility and lender policies affect qualifying. Confirm final costs and terms with a mortgage professional or real-estate lawyer.
