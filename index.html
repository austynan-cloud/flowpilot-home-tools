How this calculator works

The calculator subtracts your down payment from the home price to estimate your mortgage principal. It converts the annual quoted rate using Canadian semi-annual compounding, then calculates an equal payment for the chosen amortization and payment frequency. It assumes the interest rate stays unchanged throughout the amortization, which is not how most Canadian mortgages work after renewal.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include mortgage insurance?

No. Mortgage default insurance may be required depending on the down payment, purchase price, and eligibility. Ask your lender for a complete quote.

Are biweekly payments accelerated?

No. The biweekly option shows 26 regular payments per year. Accelerated biweekly plans use a different calculation and can pay down principal faster.

Can I rely on this for a purchase decision?

Use it for rough planning only. Confirm financing terms, qualification rules, and total housing costs with a qualified professional.
